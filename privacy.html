AKILI Privacy Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026.

AKILI is a private tool used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's Google Drive solely to read files the owner places there and to move old AKILI files to the Drive Trash.

AKILI does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. Data stays on the owner's own computer and Google account. AKILI's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The owner can revoke AKILI's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: use the support email shown on AKILI's Google sign-in screen.
